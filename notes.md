## Playtest notes:
- Green, Red and Blue common decks seemed a bit underpowered and kind of uninteresting, at least in 1 game each. Black was ok
- In Quest cards it's important to have the final outcome achievable and in your control. Nakaz are fun the opposite way: you're making a bet that it won't happen.
- White and blue not having enough flyers


## Card graphic design:
We don't have to follow the current card frame template, just the general layout. But it's probably a good idea to have lighter background for all the text fields, so they read better

Each color's frame should be a texture, probably generated with the same model.

* Green: Fur-tree branches, filling the frame with green needles
* Blue: Blue water surface with ripples.
* Black: Just black dirt or peat.
* Red: Red granite. Or maybe flames
* White: white plaster.
* Land: a few ideas: brown dirt, yellow grass of steppe
* Artifact: varnished wood of brown hue
* Multicolored: gold and gems
